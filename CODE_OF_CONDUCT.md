# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions focused on the work. Harassment,
discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, discussions, pull requests or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behavior to **destek@nafru.com**. All reports are reviewed and handled confidentially; maintainers
may remove content and restrict participation for anyone who violates this code.
